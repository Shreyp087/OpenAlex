01

Read the actual sources.

Fetch the current OpenAlex work and DOI registration metadata. Crossref first; DataCite when Crossref returns 404. Timeouts and missing records stay visible.

02

Separate facts from inference.

Compare normalized titles, show author overlap and source DOI relationships. Shared citation targets never count as identity evidence. Version relationships are review hints.

03

Export an evidence packet.

Download the findings, source responses, retrieval times, and SHA-256 hashes. Hand a maintainer a reproducible case without proposing an unsafe automatic edit.

ESTABLISHED

The sources disagree.

In the captured case, the main DOI’s registered title differs from OpenAlex. Other DOI locations represent different author groups and publications. Publisher pages corroborate the two Dagstuhl titles.

HYPOTHESIS

A relation may have been misread.

All five DOI sources cite the same arXiv paper. That is a lead to investigate, not proof of OpenAlex’s internal matching logic or the cause of this record.

DELIVERED

Live checks, captured real examples, a bounded cohort audit, executable tests, and a documented evidence trail. Upstream correction and safe splitting remain maintainer decisions.
