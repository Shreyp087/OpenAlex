A.

Why this problem?

The role connects ingestion, data quality, APIs, and support. This follows one issue across that loop, with a runnable Python engine behind it.

B.

Why no model call?

These fixtures test invariants. A model could propose a mapping later; it would still need source evidence, an abstention path, and reviewed evaluation cases.

C.

What changes at scale?

Partitioned storage, incremental checks, versioned rules, and shadow evaluation. SQLite establishes the local contract, not distributed throughput.
